# AI4IO

This repository contains two markdown files and one pdf file. All three are prepared in preparation of my PhD defence on 7 September 2026 at NTNU, Norway. For more details about my PhD defence, see [here](https://www.ntnu.no/kalender/detaljer/-/event/3a196bd5-f202-351f-88b4-a1a3b7062d01). My dissertation - titled "A world of *ifs and ans* and the assumptions behind: Modelling hypothetical structural change in environmentally-extended input-output economics" - can be accessed [here](https://hdl.handle.net/11250/5563271).

The markdown files address the **opportunities of artificial intelligence for environmentally-extended input-output analysis**. This was the topic given to me for my trial lecture, to be held before my PhD defence. Both markdown files were generated using an LLM pipeline. The report is a synthesis of 12 individual reports generated with different LLMs, where each LLM received the same instructions; the 12 reports were then merged into one of them (the one that looked most promising at brief screening). The synthesised report was subsequently converted into article format, where the focus narrowed down on the literature review. That article was revised three times (following three LLM-powered review rounds); the final version is the one included here.

The pdf file addresses the research question of **how the global economy's structure changed in the last three decades** (also in environmental terms). This research project is the result of prompting with GPT-6 Astra for in total about two hours.

I do not claim correctness of the information provided in either file, as comprehensive human fact-checking has not been part of the process yet. Similarly, I apologise for the poor writing style - it is not mine, though :wink:

If you think this work is useful, perhaps as starting point for an actual research article, please let me know so that we can figure out something!
